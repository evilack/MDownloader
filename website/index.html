Good to know

A few things to know.

Can I download videos from every site?

Availability depends on how a site delivers media, your sign-in status and access permissions. DRM-protected content and unsupported encryption or stream structures cannot be downloaded. HLS with accessible identity AES keys is supported.

Which formats are supported?

Direct video links such as MP4 and WebM, audio links such as MP3 and M4A, and supported clear or AES-encrypted HLS and unencrypted DASH streams. Compatibility depends on the codec and stream structure.

Why does it need site access?

Finding media requests and downloading files may require access to the site and its media servers. Manage site access in your browser’s extension settings.

Do I need to contribute to use it?

No. Contributions are optional and do not change the available features. You are welcome to contribute if you would like to support development.
