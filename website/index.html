Getting started

How to use MDownloader

Requires Chrome 116 or later, or a compatible current Edge. Basic media downloads run in the browser. Speech captions and some file integration features use an optional helper.

  1. 01

    Allow access and play

    Allow access under Site permissions in Options, then play the media.

  2. 02

    Choose options and save

    Click Download to save, or Shift + Download to choose quality and subtitles.

Your download, your timing

Save the portion you need.
When you need it.

01 / START

Choose a live starting point

For supported HLS, start from the earliest segment still available or near the current live edge. DVR covers only the rewind range supplied by the server; expired segments cannot be recovered.

02 / SNAPSHOT

Save received portion

Export an available playable portion as a separate partial file while the original download continues. Availability depends on the source format and data already received.

03 / FINISH

Stop and save

End new reception for a supported job and save the playable portion. Cancel instead stops the job and discards unfinished temporary output.

Supported streams can continue after the source tab closes once independent reception has started. Keep the browser running. Automatic recovery after browser shutdown or extension reload is not available.

Optional local speech captions

No subtitles?
Start with the speech.

Speech captions are off by default. Recognition runs locally. If you enable an external translation service, subtitle text is sent to that service.

When the download finishes or you choose Stop and save, captions are saved automatically in your selected embedded, SRT or VTT formats. Follow progress and results in the download row.

  1. 01

    Prepare the helper and model.

    Check the connection in the Helper tab in Options, then prepare an engine and model in Speech caption settings. Windows offers faster-whisper and whisper.cpp; macOS is configured for whisper.cpp. Download the models you need explicitly.

  2. 02

    Choose recognition and output.

    Select a speech language, Fast, Balanced or Accuracy preset, and individual recognition options. Embed captions in the container, export SRT or VTT, or combine outputs. Translation requires a configured service and credentials.

  3. 03

    Adjust each download.

    Shift-click Download to inherit defaults, turn speech captions on or off, or use custom settings. Embedded and final subtitle or translation outputs are saved after preparation. Caption progress and errors are shown separately.

Good to know

A few things to know.

Can I download videos from every site?

Availability depends on media delivery, sign-in status, permissions and codecs. DRM-protected content, unsupported encryption and live DASH are not supported. Compatible HLS with accessible identity AES keys can be processed.

Which formats are supported?

Direct video files such as MP4 and WebM, audio such as MP3 and M4A, supported HLS and static unencrypted DASH, and WebRTC from configured signaling services. This does not record every playing video on every site.

How do embedded subtitles and speech captions differ?

Original subtitle embedding fetches supported captions supplied with the media and adds them to MP4. Speech captions recognize audio using a separately prepared local engine and model. Translation uses your configured external service, whose authentication and pricing terms may apply.

Do I need to install another program?

Basic downloads do not need a separate downloader. Local speech captions use an optional helper, engine and model. On Windows, Show in folder for files saved directly to a chosen folder also needs the helper and an initial folder connection. Browser or operating system confirmations may appear during setup.

What if a download fails?

Check page and media server access, sign-in status and expired links, then play or refresh the page and retry. The extension’s issue report lets you review and send the page address and issue types. A failed preview alone does not mean the download will fail.
